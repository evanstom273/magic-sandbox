# Element Codex

Every element is two things: something you cast, and something in the world that reacts. When a spell mixes elements, or materials meet, the world decides what happens, by these rules (`GAME_DESIGN.md` #19, #20, #21).

**Status: proposals.** Lyra picked the elements (#70, #71); **everything written about them here is Claude's proposal**, waiting on her review. Decisions made from it are recorded in `GAME_DESIGN.md`; this file is kept in step with the Element Codex artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/DV9k7rdGQ1bbkGHdZbkVdL

## Elements

### Forces

#### Life (Lyra's element)

Colour: `#4fdc6a`

- **In the world:** Plants grow and spread where there's soil, water and light; everything alive carries it; rare Life springs glow in hidden places.
- **Cast:** Heals anything living it touches and mends their marks; grows plants on wet ground, sand, stone and wood; summons plants, little creatures, elemental creatures (Life mixed with another element) or wakes up material that's there.
- **Draw it in from:** Plants, creatures (drains them a little), Life springs.

#### Heat

Colour: `#ff8a2a`

- **In the world:** Fire, embers and lava give it off; it spreads to anything flammable.
- **Cast:** Ignites wood, plants, oil and gunpowder; boils water to steam; melts ice; with sustained heat turns sand to glass, metal to molten metal and stone to lava. Burns creatures, eating their cells.
- **Draw it in from:** Fire, lava (drawing heat cools lava to stone).

#### Cold

Colour: `#bfeaff`

- **In the world:** Ice, and cold caverns where water freezes on its own.
- **Cast:** Freezes water into ice (bridges, platforms, plugs); turns lava to obsidian and molten metal back to metal; condenses steam into rain; puts fire out. Slows creatures, then freezes them solid.
- **Draw it in from:** Ice (drawing cold melts it).

#### Lightning

Colour: `#fff27a`

- **In the world:** Storm crystals that crackle, and charged ore.
- **Cast:** Arcs along water, acid and metal to everything they touch; ignites flammables and sets off gunpowder; fuses sand to glass where it strikes. Stuns creatures, wet ones for longer.
- **Draw it in from:** Storm crystals, charged ore.

#### Force

Colour: `#d6d6e6`

- **In the world:** Gusts from vents and cracks that push loose stuff about.
- **Cast:** Shoves loose cells (liquids, powders, gases) and creatures away; strong enough, breaks stone to rubble and shatters glass and ice. Cast on yourself it's movement: a launch, a levitation, a dash.
- **Draw it in from:** Vents.

#### Decay

Colour: `#9aa63a`

- **In the world:** Rot and fungus that creep across wood and plants, giving off miasma.
- **Cast:** Withers plants and rots wood to rot (then dust); corrodes metal to rubble, slowly; poisons creatures, rotting their cells away over time. Life and Decay cancel where they meet.
- **Draw it in from:** Rot, fungus, miasma.

#### Light

Colour: `#fff6c8`

- **In the world:** Glowing crystals, and glow-moss (where Life and Light meet). The world is dark; light is precious.
- **Cast:** Lights up the dark around where it lands, for a while; plants grow faster in it; makes glass and ice glow; blinds creatures and scorches dark ones. A sustained beam warms what it hits.
- **Draw it in from:** Glowing crystals, glow-moss.

#### Gravity

Colour: `#8a6cff`

- **In the world:** Odd lodestones that tug at loose things around them.
- **Cast:** Pulls loose cells and creatures towards a point, or lifts them: draw a pool of water up into a floating ball, haul sand into a heap, compact sand into stone with enough of it.
- **Draw it in from:** Lodestones.

#### Void

Colour: `#c03aff` (speckled)

- **In the world:** Rifts: rare, dangerous tears that eat what drifts into them.
- **Cast:** Unmakes the cells it touches, digging clean holes through anything but obsidian; cast on yourself, it moves you (a blink). Eats creatures' cells, yours included if you're careless.
- **Draw it in from:** Rifts.

### Liquids

#### Water

Colour: `#3a86ee`

- **In the world:** Pools, streams, drips from wet ceilings.
- **Cast:** Makes water: floods, fills, washes things away. Puts out fire, conducts lightning, freezes to ice, boils to steam, feeds plants, breaks falls, dilutes acid.
- **Draw it in from:** Any water.

#### Oil

Colour: `#5a4428`

- **In the world:** Dark seeps and pools; floats on water.
- **Cast:** Makes oil: a slick that floats on water and burns long, spreading fire across a pool.
- **Draw it in from:** Oil seeps.

#### Lava

Colour: `#ff6a1e`

- **In the world:** Slow pools and flows deep down; lights its surroundings.
- **Cast:** Makes lava: slow, burning, sets anything flammable alight. Water turns it to obsidian (with steam), cold to obsidian, time and drawing its heat to stone.
- **Draw it in from:** Lava (as a substance; its heat is Heat).

#### Acid

Colour: `#c8ff3a`

- **In the world:** Bubbling pools in old places, eating slowly at their banks.
- **Cast:** Makes acid: eats stone, metal, sand, wood, plants and bodies cell by cell, used up as it goes, giving off smoke. Obsidian and glass resist it; water dilutes it.
- **Draw it in from:** Acid pools.

### Solids and powders

#### Sand

Colour: `#e0bf6e`

- **In the world:** Dunes, drifts and loose ceilings that pour when disturbed.
- **Cast:** Makes sand: falls, piles, buries, plugs gaps, cushions falls. Heat turns it to glass; lightning fuses it where it strikes; enough gravity packs it into stone.
- **Draw it in from:** Sand.

#### Stone

Colour: `#7a7486`

- **In the world:** Most of the world.
- **Cast:** Makes stone: walls, bridges, stairs, plugs. Force breaks it to rubble; acid eats it; enough heat melts it to lava.
- **Draw it in from:** Stone.

#### Gunpowder

Colour: `#4a4652` (speckled)

- **In the world:** Seams in the rock and old caches.
- **Cast:** Makes gunpowder: a trail or a heap that explodes with heat, fire, lava or lightning, blasting terrain and anything nearby.
- **Draw it in from:** Gunpowder.

#### Metal

Colour: `#9aa4b0`

- **In the world:** Ore veins and old structures.
- **Cast:** Makes metal: heavy and tough (force won't break it); conducts lightning; sustained heat melts it to molten metal, which sets again; decay and acid wear it down slowly.
- **Draw it in from:** Metal.

### Made by reactions

#### Steam

Colour: `#c8d0dc`

- **In the world:** Water plus heat. Rises, scalds creatures, condenses back to water on cold or after a while (rain from the ceiling).
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: a scalding cloud that rises.
- **Draw it in from:** Steam.

#### Smoke

Colour: `#4a4a56`

- **In the world:** Anything burning, and acid eating. Rises, hides what's behind it, fades.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: a cloud to hide in or behind.
- **Draw it in from:** Smoke.

#### Fire

Colour: `#ffb03a`

- **In the world:** Burning cells. Spreads along wood, plants and oil; rises; dies to smoke; water puts it out.
- **Cast:** Not cast directly: Heat on anything flammable makes it.
- **Draw it in from:** Gives Heat.

#### Ice

Colour: `#a8dcf0`

- **In the world:** Water plus cold. Solid and slippery; melts with heat; force shatters it.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: instant platforms and walls.
- **Draw it in from:** Ice (also gives Cold).

#### Obsidian

Colour: `#2e2040`

- **In the world:** Lava plus water or cold. The toughest thing: resists acid, void and almost all force.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: an expensive, near-unbreakable wall.
- **Draw it in from:** Obsidian.

#### Glass

Colour: `#cfe8ef`

- **In the world:** Sand plus heat or lightning. Clear, brittle (force shatters it back to sand), resists acid, lets light through and glows with it.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: see-through walls and windows.
- **Draw it in from:** Glass.

#### Rubble

Colour: `#6a6474`

- **In the world:** Stone broken by force; corroded metal. A coarse powder that falls and piles like sand.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: like sand, but it won't melt to glass.
- **Draw it in from:** Rubble.

#### Molten metal

Colour: `#ffd27a`

- **In the world:** Metal plus sustained heat. A heavy, burning liquid that sets back into metal (fast with water or cold).
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: pour it into a gap and let it set.
- **Draw it in from:** Molten metal.

#### Plants and wood

Colour: `#4fae4a`

- **In the world:** Life's doing: grass, moss, vines, roots and trees. Grow with water and light; burn; rot with decay. Moss softens falls.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in, or grown with Life.
- **Draw it in from:** Plants and wood (also give Life).

#### Rot and miasma

Colour: `#7a8a3a`

- **In the world:** Decay's leftovers: rot creeps across wood and plants; miasma is a low poison gas that hurts the living. Life clears both; fire burns miasma off.
- **Cast:** Castable once drawn in: a lingering poison cloud.
- **Draw it in from:** Gives Decay.

## When materials meet

What happens on contact, with no spell involved.

- **Lava + water:** Obsidian and a burst of steam.
- **Lava + ice:** Ice melts to water (then that water meets the lava).
- **Lava + wood, plants or oil:** They catch fire.
- **Lava + gunpowder:** Explodes.
- **Lava + metal:** Metal slowly melts to molten metal.
- **Lava + sand:** Sand slowly turns to glass.
- **Fire + wood, plants or oil:** Fire spreads into them.
- **Fire + water:** Fire goes out with a puff of steam.
- **Fire + ice:** Ice melts.
- **Fire + gunpowder:** Explodes.
- **Fire + miasma:** Miasma burns off.
- **Acid + stone, metal, sand, rubble, wood, plants or bodies:** Eaten cell by cell; the acid is used up as it goes and gives off smoke.
- **Acid + water:** The acid is diluted: it slowly turns to water.
- **Acid + obsidian or glass:** Nothing: they hold it.
- **Water + plants:** Plants grow; dry plants wither slowly.
- **Water + molten metal:** Sets into metal, with steam.
- **Oil + water:** Oil floats on top.
- **Sand or rubble + any liquid:** Sinks through it.
- **Steam + a ceiling or cold:** Condenses into water drops: rain.
- **Rot + wood or plants:** Rot spreads into them.
- **Life + rot or miasma:** Cleared.
- **Storm crystal + water:** Every so often, the water is electrified.
- **A creature + steam, fire or lava:** Burned: cells eaten.
- **A creature + Life spring:** Healed.

## Notes

- Colours stay clear of the style guide's damage red and reward gold, and of the hero's white, so nothing in the world borrows those jobs.
- Creatures (and the player) are made of cells, so fire, acid, decay and void eat their cells away, and Life mends them.
- Miasma and rot are new: they're what decay leaves behind. Snow, rust and anything else not listed would be new elements, not proposed here.
- Numbers (how fast, how far, how long) come later, in play.
