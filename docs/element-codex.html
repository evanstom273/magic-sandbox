Element Codex

Every element is two things: something you cast, and something in the world that reacts. When a spell mixes elements, or materials meet, the world decides what happens, by these rules. Lyra picked the elements; everything written about them here is Claude's proposal, to strike or change.

Notes on the proposals. Colours stay clear of the style guide's damage red and reward gold, and of the hero's white, so nothing in the world borrows those jobs. Creatures (and the player) are made of cells, so fire, acid, decay and void eat their cells away, and Life mends them. Miasma and rot are new: they're what decay leaves behind. Snow, rust and anything else not listed would be new elements, not proposed here. Numbers (how fast, how far, how long) come later, in play.
