Element Codex

Every element is two things: something you cast, and something in the world that reacts. When a spell mixes elements, or materials meet, the world decides what happens, by these rules. Lyra picked all 59 elements. She approved the first 27 as written here; the 32 added in round two are her picks with Claude's details, to strike or change.

What each force does to each material

Cast a force at something and this is what happens. "Sustained" means it takes a held spell (a Beam, a channelled Cone), not a single bolt. Highlight a force by tapping its card above. The materials added in round two describe their own reactions on their cards and below.

When materials meet

What happens on contact, with no spell involved. These are what make the world react on its own.

Notes on the proposals. Colours stay clear of the style guide's damage red and reward gold, and of the hero's white, so nothing in the world borrows those jobs. Creatures (and the player) are made of cells, so fire, acid, decay and void eat their cells away, and Life mends them. Miasma and rot are what decay leaves behind. Blood is a dark red, kept off the damage red; snow and salt are kept off the hero's white; gold is in reward gold on purpose, because it's treasure. Anything not listed would be a new element. Numbers (how fast, how far, how long) come later, in play.
